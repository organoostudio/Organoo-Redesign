The honest answer to "how much does a website cost in Indonesia?" is that it depends on what the website has to do. As of October 2026, most business websites built by a professional team fall between **Rp3 million and Rp100 million**, and the price is driven far more by scope, content and integrations than by the number of pages.

This guide breaks down typical prices by website type, explains what you are actually paying for, lists the running costs people forget, and gives you a simple way to scope a project so the budget goes into the parts that bring in customers.

## How much does a website cost in Indonesia in 2026?

A simple landing page usually costs Rp3–8 million, a company profile Rp8–25 million, an online store Rp25–100 million, and a custom web application Rp100–500 million. Those ranges come from published 2026 price guides by Indonesian developers, such as [this breakdown from Next IT](https://next-it.co.id/blog/berapa-biaya-buat-website-profesional-di-2026-panduan-lengkap-untuk-pemilik-bisnis). Treat them as a starting point for comparing quotes, not a fixed price list.

| Website type | What it usually includes | Typical price (IDR) | Approx. USD |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Landing page | One long page for a product, event or ad campaign, a contact or WhatsApp button | Rp3–8 million | $180–480 |
| Company profile | 5–10 pages: home, about, services, portfolio, blog, contact | Rp8–25 million | $480–1,500 |
| Online store | Product catalogue, cart, payment gateway, shipping rates, order admin | Rp25–100 million | $1,500–6,000 |
| Custom web app | Logins, dashboards, bookings, integrations with your own systems | Rp100–500 million+ | $6,000–30,000+ |

USD figures are rough conversions at about Rp16,500 per dollar and are only there for orientation.

The ranges are wide on purpose. Two "company profile" briefs can differ by a factor of three once you count copywriting, photography, the number of languages, a blog, and how much of the design is custom.

## What actually drives the price of a website?

Scope and custom work drive the price, not page count. The biggest cost factors are:

1. **Custom design vs. template.** A designer creating a layout around your brand takes more time than adapting a ready-made theme, but it is also what makes a site look like *your* business rather than everyone else's.
2. **Content.** Writing the copy, translating it, and producing photos or video often takes as long as building the site. If you supply finished text and images, the price drops.
3. **Features and integrations.** Payment gateways, booking calendars, CRM or marketplace connections, member areas and multi-language support each add development and testing time.
4. **Platform.** WordPress or a website builder is quicker to set up; a custom-coded site is faster to load and more flexible, but costs more to build.
5. **Speed, SEO and analytics setup.** Technical SEO, structured data, tracking pixels for Meta and Google Ads, and performance tuning are invisible but decide whether the site earns its cost back.
6. **Who builds it.** A freelancer, a small studio and a large agency price differently because they carry different overheads and offer different levels of strategy, project management and support.

### Freelancer, agency or website builder?

Each option fits a different stage of business. Here is how they compare:

| Option | Best for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DIY website builder | Testing an idea on a very small budget | Your time, generic templates, limited SEO control |
| Freelancer | A simple site with a clear brief | Availability, no backup if they disappear, ongoing support |
| Digital agency | A site tied to marketing goals, with design, copy and ads under one roof | Higher price; check that you own the domain and the code |

## What are the yearly running costs of a website?

Expect to spend roughly Rp1–5 million a year to keep a standard business website online, before any marketing. These costs are easy to forget when comparing quotes:

- **Domain name:** a .com costs from about Rp140,000 a year according to [Hostinger Indonesia's 2026 cost guide](https://www.hostinger.com/id/tutorial/estimasi-biaya-pembuatan-website/), while renewals and local extensions like .co.id usually land between Rp200,000 and Rp500,000 a year.
- **Hosting:** shared and WordPress hosting starts around Rp25,000 a month; a busier site or online store needs more. Modern static sites can often run on very low-cost or free edge hosting.
- **SSL certificate:** free with most hosts today. Avoid any provider that still charges for basic SSL.
- **Maintenance:** updates, backups, security patches and small content changes typically cost 10–15% of the build price per year, or a monthly retainer.
- **Paid plugins and themes:** for WordPress sites, premium plugins can add Rp500,000–Rp2.5 million a year.

Ask every vendor to list these separately. A cheap build with an expensive, locked-in hosting package often costs more over three years than a fairly priced build you fully own.

## Why do two quotes for the same website differ so much?

Because they are rarely quoting the same thing. When one agency quotes Rp7 million and another Rp30 million for a "company profile", the difference is usually hidden in what is included:

- Is the copy written for you, or do you have to write it?
- Is the design custom or a modified template?
- How many rounds of revisions are included?
- Is basic SEO set up (titles, descriptions, sitemap, structured data, page speed)?
- Are Google Analytics, Search Console and ad pixels installed?
- Who owns the domain, hosting account and source code at the end?
- What happens after launch: is there training, a support period, a maintenance plan?

Put the quotes side by side against this list and the "expensive" one is sometimes the cheaper deal.

## How to scope your website so you pay for what matters

Start from the goal, not the page list. A website that exists to collect WhatsApp leads from Meta Ads needs a very different budget split from one that must rank on Google for ten service keywords.

1. **Write down the one job the site must do.** Generate leads, sell products online, build credibility for tenders, or recruit staff.
2. **List the pages that serve that job,** and drop the rest. Many small businesses only need four or five strong pages.
3. **Decide who writes the content.** If you write it, budget your own time; if the agency writes it, budget for it.
4. **Separate "launch" from "later".** A blog, a second language or a member area can come in phase two once the site is earning.
5. **Ask for the running costs in writing,** including renewal prices, not only first-year promos.
6. **Insist on owning your domain and accounts.** They should be registered in your company's name.

Whoever builds your site, agree on this goal-first brief before design starts, because it decides where the budget should go: design, content, speed, or the integrations behind the scenes. It is also the first thing we ask about in every [Organoo website project](/#service-web).

## Is a cheap website worth it?

A cheap website is worth it only if it still does its job. A Rp2 million site that loads slowly on mobile, has no clear call to action and cannot be found on Google costs you leads every month. Google's research on mobile pages found that [as load time goes from one to ten seconds, the probability of a visitor bouncing rises by 123%](https://business.google.com/ca-en/think/marketing-strategies/mobile-page-speed-new-industry-benchmarks/).

On the other hand, there is no reason for a new business to start with a Rp100 million build. A focused landing page or a small, fast company profile is often the smartest first step, as long as it is built on something you can grow later rather than throw away.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a company profile website cost in Indonesia?

A professional company profile website with 5–10 pages typically costs Rp8–25 million in 2026. The price rises with custom design, copywriting, extra languages and features such as a blog or portfolio filters. Templates and client-supplied content bring it toward the lower end.

### How long does it take to build a business website?

A landing page usually takes one to two weeks, a company profile three to six weeks, and an online store six to twelve weeks. The biggest source of delay is usually content, so having text and photos ready shortens the timeline more than anything else.

### Do I need to pay for a website every year?

Yes, but the yearly cost is small compared with the build. You renew the domain and hosting every year, roughly Rp1–5 million in total for a standard site, and you may choose a maintenance plan for updates, backups and security.

### Should I use WordPress or a custom-coded website?

WordPress is a good fit when you want to edit many pages yourself and use ready-made plugins. A custom-coded or static site is a better fit when speed, security and low running costs matter most. Both can rank well on Google if they are built properly.

### Who should own the domain and hosting?

You should. Register the domain and hosting accounts in your company's name, and give your developer access rather than ownership. That way you can change vendors at any time without losing your website or email.

## The bottom line

In Indonesia, a good business website in 2026 costs anywhere from a few million rupiah for a landing page to well over Rp100 million for a custom platform.
